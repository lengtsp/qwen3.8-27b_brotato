# Limitations and unresolved issues

- **Player versus weapons:** changing equipment, outfits, flashes and crowded pixels can prevent a reliable current-body match. A successful frozen-menu inspection does not guarantee tracking after Resume.
- **Recovery is not normal planning:** an enabled exploratory fallback can keep sending bounded directions while body localization fails. That does not establish collision-free routes or reliable collection. In the recent 12-second recovery observation, normal actor-grounded steps remained zero.
- **Corner search is a search prior:** an experimental down-right action sequence cannot guarantee arrival at the lower-right corner. Speed, walls, camera motion and interruptions require current-image confirmation.
- **Stale model outputs:** image capture, CPU checks, GPU inference and input delivery have different clocks. Expired advice is rejected; that can leave control on the local fallback. Input ACKs do not independently prove every rendered displacement.
- **Memory coverage:**28 audited visual cases contain no positive interval HP-drop and no complete build labels. They cannot establish learning from damage or independent-run generalization.
- **Unproven learning:** Qwen weights remain fixed. Reflections are drafts and case retrieval is advisory. Neither larger memory nor repeated play proves self-improvement.
- **Limited comparisons:** uncontrolled encounter RNG, tiny gameplay samples and partially observed segments prevent causal score claims. Held-out model accuracy, reliable autonomous win rate and end-to-end concurrent latency remain unmeasured.
- **Prior failures:**19 older busy/unavailable offers remain unexplained. The later 120/120 service probe does not resolve their historical cause.
- **Memory/FPS scope:** short component memory tests do not prove that the full game has no leak. Game FPS, capture FPS, preview FPS and command rate must be measured separately.
- **Human ownership:** character, weapon, difficulty, level-up, shop and item decisions remain with the operator. This prototype has not demonstrated dependable fully autonomous play.

- **Crop and overlay limits:** a wrong crop can omit the actor, and an overlay can display an incorrect prediction. A clearer explanation on screen does not establish better detection or gameplay. Their separate effects have not been measured.
